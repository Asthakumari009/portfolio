How I work

A four-step loop from idea to something running in production.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Define the user, the constraint, and the smallest surface area that validates the hypothesis.

  2. 02

    Architect

    Map data flows, pick the leanest stack, and design for the failure modes, not the happy path.

  3. 03

    Build

    Ship in tight iterations. Production prototypes meet real users early; feedback shapes v1, not v10.

  4. 04

    Deploy

    Launch under live constraints, with observability, performance budgets, and recoverability built in.
